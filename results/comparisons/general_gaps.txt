General model, gap filling: scores on the hidden span and paired differences.
Differences carry a 95% interval from a bootstrap over the 144 test patients (1,000 resamples).
ABP in mmHg; PPG and ECG in units of each segment's standard deviation.
The script reads the per-segment scores (*.npz) and the patient codes, which are not in this repository.
